An Iconic Financial Landmark in Tay Ho Tay's Emerging Economic Hub

Rising at the heart of Starlake Tay Ho Tay, IFC Hanoi is set to redefine the city's skyline and establish a new benchmark for integrated commercial developments in Vietnam's capital. More than a building, it represents a bold vision for what Hanoi can become - a city that stands shoulder to shoulder with the great commercial capitals of Asia.

Distinguished by its contemporary architecture and premium-grade specifications, the development brings together Grade A offices, luxury retail, and world-class hospitality within a single landmark destination. Every detail has been considered - from the sweeping lobby atriums to the precision-engineered curtain walls that frame panoramic views of West Lake and the emerging Tay Ho Tay skyline. It is designed not only to meet international standards but to set them, anchoring Hanoi's fastest-growing financial and business district with a presence that commands attention.

The office towers offer expansive, column-free floor plates built for the demands of multinational corporations, financial institutions, and forward-thinking enterprises seeking a prestigious Hanoi address. Flexible layouts, advanced building management systems, and sustainability credentials make IFC Hanoi a workspace designed for the next generation of business.

At its core, the Shilla Monogram hotel delivers an elevated hospitality experience unlike anything currently available in the city. Operated by South Korea's premier hospitality group, Hotel Shilla, it combines refined Korean service traditions with effortless modern sophistication. From meticulously curated interiors to signature dining and wellness offerings, every touchpoint is designed to leave a lasting impression on discerning travelers and executive guests alike.

The retail podium completes the vision - a curated destination for luxury and lifestyle brands, dining, and cultural experiences that will draw visitors from across the city and beyond. Together, these elements create a self-contained ecosystem where business, leisure, and living converge seamlessly.

IFC Hanoi isn't just a landmark. It's a statement - one that signals Hanoi's arrival on the global stage and invites the world to take notice.
