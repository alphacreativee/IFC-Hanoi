IFC / About Us
The Office Tower at IFC Hanoi is a modern workspace featuring retail at its base and award-winning office floors above, recognized for excellence in design and functionality.

vision

To become Hanoi's leading integrated business destination, setting a new standard for how businesses work, connect, and thrive through the seamless integration of workplace, hospitality, and retail experiences.

mission

To deliver an elevated premium workplace experience by bringing together Grade A offices, hospitality, and everyday conveniences within one connected destination that supports business performance, corporate image, and talent attraction.
core value

Creating an environment where people can work, connect, and thrive
